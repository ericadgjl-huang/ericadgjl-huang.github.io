Assignment 1: AI Use Log

This page documents my use of generative AI for EPPS 6356 Assignment 1, following the course AI policy.

Tool Information

Item Details
Tool Claude (Anthropic), used in the Claude desktop app (Cowork mode)
Date September 15, 2026
Language I asked questions in Chinese (Traditional). My original prompts are quoted below with English translations. Claude’s replies are summarized in English.

How and Why I Used AI

  • How: step-by-step guidance for setting up RStudio projects, running Fall.R and anscombe01.R, exporting figures, and adding a page to my Quarto website. I also used it to polish and translate my English writing, suggest regression diagnostics, and calculate percentage declines for the chart critique.
  • Why: to catch up on the course after starting late, to save time with unfamiliar tools (RStudio projects, Quarto rendering), and to clarify my writing in English, which is not my first language.
  • What is my own: the choice of colors and chart, the core argument in Section 1 (summary statistics oversimplify data) and in Section 3 (length is perceived better than area; bubble charts are used because they are trendy).

Note12. Writing the Anscombe paragraph and the chart critique (most relevant)

Me: 第一題我想是統計數據會過度簡略，畢竟它是一種摘要，但實際情況還是要觀察散點圖。

For the first question, I think the statistical data might be overly simplified, since it is just a summary, but in reality, you still need to look at the scatter plot.

圖表評論來自 The Functional Art：因為人對於長短的感知相較於面積來說更好（一維好於面積），若這張圖改為 bar chart 會更好理解。但因為 bubble chart 的流行大家有點盲目跟風，所以我想我們需要重新思考我們到底需要什麼而不是只是追逐流行。

For the critique, from The Functional Art: people perceive length better than area, so a bar chart would be easier to understand. But because bubble charts are popular, everyone has been blindly following the trend. We need to rethink what we really need instead of chasing trends.

Claude:

  • Section 1: Polished my idea into a full paragraph. Added the specific statistics from my R output, described each data set’s structure, and suggested the solutions: plotting before and after modeling, residual plots, nonlinear models, Cook’s distance, and robust regression.
  • Section 3: Pointed out that my critique repeated Cairo’s own argument, so it needed an original contribution. Calculated the percentage decline for each bank from the chart’s labels (e.g., RBS −96%, Citigroup −93%, Barclays −92%, Santander −45%). Suggested that the real story is the percentage loss, which a sorted bar chart of percentage change or a dumbbell chart would show better. Drafted the English critique around my argument plus this point.
